ThriveMap

Find the countries where you can live freely as yourself.

ThriveMap is a free, open tool for LGBTQ+ people and people of colour who are thinking about relocating — or who simply want to know where in the world they would be most safe, welcome, and able to thrive.

Build a profile for one or two people, set your priorities across nine dimensions of livability, and see 77 countries ranked from best fit to worst — with per-dimension explanations and links to authoritative sources such as Equaldex, ILGA, TGEU, the WHO, the World Bank and the Global Peace Index.

Your answers never leave your browser. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. No account needed.
